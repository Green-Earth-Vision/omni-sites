Alignment Survey

For exploring whether OmniOne and your organization could build toward the same architecture — and eventually combine. Honest short answers beat polished ones. There are no wrong answers; the point is to surface the real overlaps and the real deal-breakers early, so a consortium works long-term. ~10 questions · open text unless noted.

1 · Purpose & values

  • In a sentence or two, what is your organization for? What would success look like in 20 years?
  • Is regeneration / non-harm a core design goal, or a nice-to-have?

2 · Ownership & capital

  • Who owns the organization? Can ownership be bought or sold?
  • Does capital (investors/equity) carry control or governance rights? (yes / partial / no)
  • Where does profit/surplus go — to owners, or to a commons / reinvestment?

3 · How you decide (the key one)

  • How are important decisions actually made today? (hierarchy · consensus · a named method · other)
  • Do you use a defined decision process? If so, name it.
  • For shared decisions in a consortium, would you adopt a coherence-first, non-hierarchical method (our ACT) — or do you have one you believe is better? If better: what is it, and why?

4 · Authority & roles

  • Does authority live in people (bosses) or in agreed roles / agreements?
  • Can any one person or group override the rest? Under what conditions?

5 · Economics & value flow

  • How does value move — wages, equity, contribution, access, other?
  • Is there a shared / commons pool, or is everything individually held?

6 · Anti-capture

  • What stops your organization from being captured, bought, or steered by a single interest?

7 · Conflict

  • How do you handle serious internal conflict or disagreement?

8 · Goals & overlap

  • What are you building toward that overlaps with a new, non-extractive operating system?
  • What would you most want from a consortium like this?

9 · Combining

  • Are you open to building toward a shared architecture / standard so systems can combine later? (yes / maybe / no — why)

10 · Deal-breakers

  • What is non-negotiable for you? What would make this not work?
