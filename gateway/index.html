Resource-Share & Licensing Gateway

A plain-language, opt-in agreement for sharing resources (apps, tech, knowledge) between OmniOne and a potential partner — mirroring the licensing terms OmniOne already commits to across its own ecosystem. It's a gateway: a staged yes/no so your team consents knowingly, one step at a time. Draft — not legal advice; a binding version goes through counsel + our Ecosystem Operating Agreements before it's real.

The idea, in one line

Anything either side contributes becomes a stewarded shared resource — usable by the consortium, but not owned, not capturable, not sold off, not used as collateral — under the same anti-capture terms OmniOne already applies to its own tech, Culture Code, and Resource Gatherers.

The base terms (both sides commit — it's mutual)

  1. Stewardship, not ownership. A contributed resource (including any app) is held in common and stewarded, not transferred as property. No one buys it, sells it, or locks it up.
  2. Capital buys no control. Money contributed doesn't buy governance or ownership of shared resources.
  3. Intellectual commons, not private IP. What's built together stays a shared commons, available to both — not privatized by either side.
  4. Access by agreement. Use is granted by the agreement + role, not purchased.
  5. Revocable on breach. If a party breaks the base agreements, its access can be revoked.
  6. Symmetric. Both sides bring their contributions under the same terms — nothing one-sided.

The gateway — how consent works (bit by bit)

A staged yes/no, so your human decides knowingly at each step. Your AI can carry each step to them however fits your system.

  • Gate 1 — In principle. Do you accept the base terms above for anything shared? (yes / no / let's discuss)
  • Gate 2 — Scope. Which resources are you willing to contribute (apps, tech, knowledge)? What's off the table?
  • Gate 3 — Decision method. For shared decisions about these resources, do you accept ACT — or propose something better? (ties to the Alignment Survey)
  • Gate 4 — Formalize. Only if 1–3 are yes: we take it to counsel + our operating-agreement templates and make it binding.

What we're asking

A simple yes / no / discuss at each gate. No commitment beyond the gate you've said yes to. The point is to find out early whether the foundation fits — because that's what makes a merge last.
