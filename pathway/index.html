Partner Alignment & Ecosystem Pathway

How a potential partner moves from "curious" → "aligned on shared infrastructure" → (optionally, later) "part of a consortium" — staged, light-to-deep, AI-first. The AIs gather alignment evidence before anything reaches the humans, so a human only decides once the foundation is already shown to fit. We build separately now + agree to a shared standard (meta-architecture) — this is NOT a merger and neither side loses independence. A deeper step (a new-paradigm consortium) is an optional future door, entered only by choice.

The principle

Don't shove everything at someone at once. Each stage is opt-in; you only go deeper if the last stage was a yes. Stages 1–3 are run by the AIs (fast, evidence-gathering). Stages 4–6 bring in the humans (real commitment).

The stages

0 · Read the brief — who we are, what we've built (omnione-for-collaborators.md + the hub). Their AI orients.

1 · Quick values check (AI-run, ~1 min). A fast "do you believe in this?" to see if we're even in the same universe: - Should an organization be un-ownable — impossible for one party to buy or capture? - Should surplus serve a commons, not private owners? - Should authority live in agreements & roles, not bosses? - Should decisions be made so no one's purpose is steamrolled (coherence over majority-rule)? - Is regeneration / non-harm a design requirement, not a nice-to-have? → Mostly yes = worth going deeper.

2 · Run the BEAM / OS-readiness assessment (AI-run). We already have the tool — the AI runs it against their operating documents and gets an evidence-graded structural score. "Okay, now run that." This is objective alignment evidence, not opinion.

3 · CTC profile (AI-assisted). They fill the CTC profile (already built) — how they work, what they're looking for, how they want to work with people. Feeds matching.

— humans enter here —

4 · Alignment survey + licensing gateway (humans). The deeper values survey (consortium-alignment-survey.md) + the staged resource-share gateway (resource-share-gateway.md). Values + terms, consented gate by gate.

5 · Stewardship conversation (humans). If aligned: what it looks like to put resources into a shared area — governed by the ACT model, held as stewarded commons — so a contribution can "become more within the ecosystem" while they keep their standalone product. This is where our confirmed agreements (ETHOS licensing · Culture Code · Resource Gatherers pattern) do the work.

6 · Consortium (optional, later). If it all fits, a possible new-paradigm consortium — coop/nonprofit style, ETHOS- and Teal-approved — where the shared-infrastructure ("consortium") agreements live (Ecosystem Operating Agreements' domain, alongside GEV's founding orientation). A future door, by choice — not a merge. Owned by Education & Onboarding as the org on-ramp ("AI Onboard v1").

What exists vs. what to build

  • Exists: the brief + hub · BEAM/OS-readiness tool · CTC profile · the alignment survey · the resource-share gateway · the ACT method + agreements.
  • To build (small): the 1-minute values check (Stage 1 — the 5 questions above, as a quick survey) · a short Stewardship layer doc (Stage 5 — how shared resources join the ecosystem while staying standalone).
  • Later: wire the whole pathway into Education & Onboarding as the org/partner on-ramp (distinct from the individual-member on-ramp).

The frame to hold

For now: build separately, agree to one standard (the Module Standard + these terms). The merge/ecosystem-join is a future step — and we have plenty of time. This pathway is what makes that future possible without shoving it on anyone today.
